☀️ *Morning Brief* — Sunday, Oct 12

The sharpest signal today is a billing arbitrage, not a tool. *Atticus* (Show HN, 24pts) is a Mac voice assistant that "runs on your Claude Pro or Max plan — no API key." It piggybacks a flat consumer subscription instead of metered API tokens. That's the inverse of the per-token economics everyone else is fighting, and it's the third day this week the *Claude billing friction* theme has surfaced. For anyone selling agent capability, the question is shifting from "how cheap are my tokens" to "whose subscription am I billing against."
