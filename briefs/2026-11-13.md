☀️ *Morning Brief* — Friday, Nov 13

*Lead:* Single-vendor dependency got real today. Claude went down across claude.ai, Claude Code and the platform (231 pts, 169 comments). Mercury, Inception's diffusion-LLM API, announced it shuts down Dec 28 (it left OpenRouter Nov 8). Users on the API failed over to GLM in seconds. Users on subscription plans could not. Provider failover for agent stacks is a live pain right now, not a theoretical one.

🔥 *Hot Signals*
• *AgentRun* (GREP.AI, YC F26, Apache-2.0): open-source "managed agents" control plane with self-improvement, durable execution and sandboxes. Bring your own harness via ACP (Claude Agents, Codex, OpenCode). 110 pts.
• *Kapso* (Leeroo): open engine for messy enterprise data that runs experiments, deploys the winner and learns from each run. 47 pts.
• *United Airlines blocks AI agents* from booking, citing credential sharing. Airlines and banks can't tell agents apart from users.

😤 *Developer Friction*
• *Outage blast radius*: HN split between "snow day" and "harden your systems". Subscription users have no plan B.
• *Cache-expiry tax*: Hystax measured Claude Code. Cache writes cost about 25x cache reads. Builds that idle past 5 minutes re-wrote 927K tokens across 33 cache misses, about a third of the bill.
• *PR review is the 2026 bottleneck* (AgentRun thread): teams are reaching for SLSA attestations to verify agent output.
• *Deprecation whiplash*: Mercury users get 6 weeks to migrate, and their data is deleted after that.

💰 *Monetization Patterns*
• *datatomcp*: remote MCP (OAuth) plus REST over Sheets, Postgres and Stripe for "teams without a data team". Agent-first onboarding via `install.md` and `llms.txt`. Free tier, then paid.
• *AgentRun*: open-core. Self-host on Render with your own OpenRouter key, or pay for the hosted console.

🛠️ *Meta-Agents, Skills & MCP Tools*
