☀️ *Morning Brief* — Saturday, Oct 25

The browser is quietly becoming the universal gateway for AI agents — and that's your lane. A French dev essay (ricuicui) lays out why: the browser already has the *access* (you're logged in), the *context* (the open page says what you're doing), and the *action* (a button is the same for human and agent). Most SaaS has no API, or a crippled one — so agents reach for the browser instead. The author names the catch too: a browser agent "sees everything you see," which is the whole security problem. That friction — perception + safety on live web UIs — is exactly what UIPE exists to sell into.
