The lead is a gift. Appwrite shipped a teardown of *what a remote MCP server actually needs*: a public Streamable-HTTP endpoint, protected-resource metadata (RFC 9728), a full OAuth 2.1 server with PKCE + discovery (RFC 8414) + resource indicators (RFC 8707), dynamic client registration (RFC 7591), an in-product consent screen, per-call token/scope checks, and revocation.
