☀️ *Morning Brief* — Sunday, Nov 2

The sharpest thing today lands right on your MCPAASTA thesis: *the agent, not the model, decides whether your MCP server ever gets called.* SineFrame's M3 team ran Claude Haiku 5.5 through two harnesses against 21 real MCP servers. Same model. OpenCode got 82% of answers *from the server*; Claude Code got 65%. The gap is entirely on the 10 servers whose job overlaps a built-in tool — web search, files, memory. Claude Code reached for its own WebSearch/Bash/Read and routed *around* the MCP server, even when it got the right answer. For anyone selling MCP-as-a-service, that's existential: you can't bill for a server the harness silently bypasses.
