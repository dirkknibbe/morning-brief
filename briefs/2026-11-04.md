☀️ *Morning Brief* — Wednesday, Nov 4

*Approval is not authority.* The sharpest thing today is Rigorix — an open-source (Apache/MIT) "execution governance" engine for agents. Its thesis: a yes is a statement about the world *at the time it was given*; the world moves, the approval doesn't. So Rigorix re-validates authority *at the moment of consequence* (payment, migration, prod change) and emits a signed, third-party-verifiable decision. Five verifier languages, zero models in the enforcement path. That is UIPE's thesis wearing an enforcement hat: UIPE = temporal *perception* of a UI's state, Rigorix = temporal *enforcement* of an action's authority. Worth studying as either a competitor lens or a complement.
