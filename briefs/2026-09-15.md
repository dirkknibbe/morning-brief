☀️ *Morning Brief* — Tuesday, Sep 15

The MCP-credential problem finally has a shape worth copying. *keydrisLabs/mcp-auth-keydris-template* (Show HN) ships an MCP server that holds *no* secret of its own — for every `tools/call` it redeems a single-use, action-scoped token (injected on `params._meta`) at a gateway/vault, applies the credential at the moment the outbound request fires, and forgets it. `initialize` and `tools/list` never touch the gateway, so clients connect and browse tools with zero auth. That per-call redemption is *exactly* a micropayment primitive — a token minted per action, evaluated by a proxy, is one accounting hook away from MCPAASTA metering. This is the closest thing yet to your distribution thesis shipping as open source.
