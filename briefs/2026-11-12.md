☀️ *Morning Brief* — Thursday, Nov 12

*The agent-to-legacy-website gap just got a YC-backed competitor — and an incumbent wall on the same day.* Pomerado (YC F26) launched on HN: "turn any website into a deterministic, self-healing MCP in minutes." Their agent browses a site once via Playwright/HTTP, records the run, then generates a repeatable API — a Guardian model checks it against guardrails and synthetic inputs guard against overfitting. They handle auth + 2FA without the model ever reading credentials. This is the closest thing yet to UIPE's neighborhood. Their framing is the tell: *"even if computer use were perfect, most companies have banned it for security and compliance."* On the same day, United Airlines banned AI agents from booking flights, citing security and federal-rule risk. The friction is now a business model.

🔥 *Hot Signals*
- Pomerado converts UIs into deterministic MCPs just-in-time; devs describe a workflow in natural language, get an MCP in <1hr, calls return in 5-30s.

😤 *Developer Friction*
- Computer-use is slow, expensive, unreliable *and* increasingly banned — United is the first big airline to wall off agents outright. Agents can't transact on the exact legacy sites (airlines, banks, gov, insurance) where assistants are most useful.
- Pomerado exists because builders kept hitting "the biggest bottleneck was getting agents to interface with legacy websites." That's a validated, recurring pain — not speculation.
