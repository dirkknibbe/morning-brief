☀️ *Morning Brief* — Tuesday, Oct 27

An analytics shop sent 23 AI agents (ChatGPT, Claude, Comet, Manus + 19 more) through a test site and logged every request and click. The headline for you: browser agents are *indistinguishable from a human in Chrome* — real user-agent, no automation flags, often from a home IP. The one tell that held across *every* clicking agent was **how the pointer travels to the button**. And only *one* of 23 (Manus) signed its requests so the site could verify it was an agent. That's a provenance vacuum and a perception signal sitting in the same dataset — squarely your lane.
