☀️ *Morning Brief* — Friday, Oct 23

The agent-identity wall just went up in public. Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, BofA, and Citi are now *blocking* third-party AI agents that log into customer accounts with the customer's own credentials. The banks' core complaint is the exact gap you keep circling: *they can't tell whether an agent is acting on a user's behalf or acting on its own.* No agent identity, no provenance, no temporal record of what the agent saw and did. Read-only is the ceiling they'll allow — and even that's grudging. This is the friction market forming in real time.
