☀️ *Morning Brief* — Thursday, Sep 17

*The most useful "tool for AI agents" is a thin layer on a tool agents already know.* Sean Goedecke's "Don't build tools for AI agents" (top of HN today) makes the case bluntly: tools good for agents are usually good for humans, existing software has a massive training-data moat, and nobody actually knows the ideal agent ergonomics yet. His concession is the whole opportunity — MCP servers, CLIs, and plaintext/Markdown exposure *are* worth building, but as "improvements on the margin," not net-new agent-only products. That's the exact niche UIPE lives in: agents can already read a DOM; UIPE adds the time dimension to a surface (the browser) they're already fluent in. Marginal ergonomics on a training-data-rich tool is the defensible play — a new agent-only product is not.
